## What it is

Hot-Talks is built around talking to an AI girlfriend. You choose a character from the roster, start a chat, and the companion replies in that thread. The site presents several personalities (playful, passionate, adventurous and others) so you can pick a tone instead of building everything from scratch.

It runs in the browser. No separate app is required to try the chat.

## What you can do

- **Chat.** Talk to a chosen companion any time the site is up.
- **Pick a personality.** Start from a listed character instead of a blank bot.
- **Photos.** The product page mentions photo sharing and generated images in chat. Confirm what is included on your plan.
- **Voice.** Voice messages are listed on the site. Availability can depend on the plan.
- **Memory.** The site says companions can remember details from earlier chats. Treat that as a product claim and judge it in use.

## Who it is for

Use it if you want a simple 18+ companion chat in the browser and prefer choosing a ready character. Skip it if you need therapy, a real relationship, or a general work chatbot.
